# Insights

1. The average exam score is 67.24 across 6,607 students, and average attendance is 79.98%, so typical performance sits in the upper-middle of the 0-100 range.
2. Exam scores are right-skewed (skew = 1.64): the mean (67.24) sits slightly above the median (67.00), indicating a few unusually high scores pulling the tail to the right.
3. Attendance has the strongest positive correlation with exam score (r = 0.58), followed by hours studied (r = 0.45); sleep hours are essentially uncorrelated (r = -0.02).
4. Sampling variability shrinks with sample size: means of 20 samples of 30 ranged from 66.47 to 69.30, while means of 20 samples of 500 ranged only from 67.00 to 67.51, illustrating the Central Limit Theorem.
5. Parental involvement is associated with exam performance: mean scores were High = 68.09 (n = 1,908), Medium = 67.10 (n = 3,362), and Low = 66.36 (n = 1,337), a spread of about 1.7 points between the High and Low groups.
